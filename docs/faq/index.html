FAQ

Booking rules and what to send Jay.

Book a time Call 435-531-4127
How do I book a time?

Use the schedule. Public booking is first come, first served, in Mountain Time. Online booking shows the first day you can book. For today or tomorrow, call or text Jay.

Can I book for today?

Online booking does not open today or tomorrow. Call or text Jay at 435-531-4127 if the vehicle needs someone sooner.

What does a job cost?

Ask Jay for a quote. Numbers for a job are not listed.

Where do you work?

Southwest Montana, including Bozeman, Belgrade, Manhattan, Three Forks, Ennis, Big Sky, West Yellowstone, Gardiner, Livingston, and Big Timber. Each town has its own page.

I do not have a street address. What should I send?

Use Text Jay your location. Your pin goes only into your own text. Add the nearest road, trailhead, or mile marker, plus the vehicle and what it is doing.

What is a fleet account?

A fleet account gets a PIN and a board. Fleet clients can ask for a window sooner than the public line. If that window is already taken, the screen asks you to confirm before anything moves.

Which time zone is the board in?

Mountain Time. The schedule page labels every window in that zone.
