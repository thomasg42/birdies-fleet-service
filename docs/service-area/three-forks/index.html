Mobile mechanic in Three Forks

Jay comes to the vehicle. Send the place it is parked.

Book a time Call 435-531-4127

Three Forks sits in Gallatin County where Interstate 90 meets US-287, near the Missouri Headwaters. The Jefferson, Madison, and Gallatin rivers come together just outside town. That junction is the geography people mean when they say the truck is "by the rivers," which is not yet a place Jay can find.

Name the highway, the park road, or the street. Headwaters visitors leave cars and trucks in lots that look similar from a text message. A pin plus the lot you walked away from is the difference. Local trucks are more often in a yard off US-287 or on a lane between the bench and the river bottom.

US-287 runs south toward Ennis and the Madison valley. A diesel that quit on that road may be on a shoulder with river wind and little shade. Say the direction of travel and the nearest marker. In town, a brake job or a no-crank can wait in a driveway like anywhere else.

The headwaters area also puts campers and hunting rigs on short gravel spurs. Those are backcountry-style notes even though the pavement is close: vehicle, symptom, pin. Do not assume a business name is posted at the spur.

Fleet units passing through on I-90 sometimes need a yard for one window and then leave the state. If the truck will not still be there, do not book a later day and hope. Call for a sooner conversation, or pick a window you know the unit can sit through.

Three Forks gets its own page so a headwaters pin is not filed under Bozeman or Ennis by mistake. The rivers meet here. The note should say which bank, and whether you are on the park side or the highway side of the junction. Add the lot name if a sign is posted where you parked. The river side and the highway side are not the same pin.
